## r-npc-fight (20261019-021823): start-combat, no pause, but a blind spot

start-combat returned in 0.296 s with `inCombat: true`, no console, no
pause. The old run (20261017-083447, still live, green, watching 4)
freezes one bandit bit-for-bit from t=1293 to 4809 ms, 3516 ms, before
it resumes; that is the 3.5 s console pause. This run's watched
bandit (0xff0008f1) and the player both keep taking small per-tick updates
through the same window; no frozen stretch appears anywhere.
Barely matters: that bandit's IsAttacking already reads true at the
watch's first tick, t=13 ms, nearly a second before the scripted
start-combat call fires. Both placed bandits are `aggressive: true`
and seem to have engaged on proximity alone beforehand, so there is no
"waiting for the fight to start" left to measure.

Gap: this scenario still finds actors by the random nearby search, not by
form id, and this time it missed one of the two bandits outright. Only
0xff0008f1, a skeever and a rabbit got watched (`watching: 3`); 0xff0008f0
has no track, no events, nothing, though the player's own hits-taken log
shows 9 hits total, 4 from the watched bandit (matching its own hits-dealt
list exactly) and 5 from the invisible one. The old run caught both
bandits, by luck.

For the one bandit measured: IsAttacking true for three windows (13-1611,
6562-8245, 18779-20461 ms), IsBlocking for four (3195-4245, 4528-6594,
8028-8378, 9045-13094 ms), IsRecoiling and IsStaggering never true across
the 21.4 s fight. It landed 4 hits (one power-flagged, one bash-flagged, two
plain) and ran about 11 move/stop cycles (22 moveStart, 11 moveStop),
staying within 443 u of its start. The player took all 9 hits, staggered 5 times, and its health dipped off the 3000 floor and back within
about a second each time.

## What the tools still could not see

- Embry's door-transit reads as a 50032-unit jump; `maxDisplacement` does
  not know about an interior/exterior coordinate-frame change, so a cell
  change looks identical to a teleport bug.
- The form-id fix only shipped for r-npc-packages. r-npc-fight's random
  search missed one of two bandits, the one that landed more of the hits;
  x-npc-puppet's search caught its one placed actor by luck.
- npc-state's package getter still reads the same id whether the actor runs
  its package, is puppeted, or is mid-recoil in combat; AI off still cannot
  be told from AI on by it.
- Hilde, Camilla Valerius, Lucan Valerius, Orgnar and Delphine read
  identically motionless the whole watch; nothing here tells
  simulated-and-sitting apart from an unloaded, frozen cell.
- Whether the shout's four same-millisecond hits are real or one hit
  counted four ways is still unmeasured; both readings only show it
  repeats.
