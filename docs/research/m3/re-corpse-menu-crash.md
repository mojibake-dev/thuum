## 3. A body's InventoryChanges against a chest's

The same structures (InventoryChanges -> BSSimpleList<InventoryEntryData*>; an entry is the object plus a list of ExtraDataList*). Three differences matter. (a) A body's worn stacks each own an ExtraDataList (ExtraWorn 0x16 / ExtraWornLeft 0x17, often ExtraLeveledItem 0x2E and ExtraOwnership); a chest's entries mostly have `extraLists == nullptr`, and every per-row reader (the name 16018, the equip state 51805 through 16003, the card's 14563/14564) dereferences a list only when one exists: a stale entry is silent on a chest and fatal on a body. (b) `Actor::RemoveItem` (37797) and `Actor::AddObjectToContainer` (37525) add the equipment handling (`ActorEquipManager::UnequipObject` 38901 / `EquipObject` 38894, Offsets.h:25-27) that splits, merges and frees lists with NO UI message. (c) The fork treats a body differently on purpose: formView's dead-body branch (0831dfe1 formView.ts:656-682) applies the server's list under the open ContainerMenu as a diff alone (`applyModelInventory(refr, inv, !deadBody)`, resetBaseFirst false: no `removeAllItems`, hence none of Skyrim Souls' two nulls either), and `ItemMenuRefresh::Shows` (ItemMenuRefresh.cpp) returns false for ANY Actor owner, so `refreshContainerMenu` posts nothing for a body (its log line reads "not posted"; the same line stands for the chest 371e9 at 19:03 and 20:38 in the other session's platform logs, so the handle comparison may fail for chests as well). The engine's `TESObjectREFR::RemoveItem` (19689; the AddItemEx negative-delta call, PapyrusTESModPlatform.cpp:777) posts only `{body, item}`, the dirty shape the menu queues without redrawing (the earlier read's trap 1); `AddObjectToContainer` posts nothing; the Papyrus RemoveAllItems native (56215, 0xa2f620) ends with no null either (441567 then the HUD line), which is why Souls hooks it. The row update trips on nothing: it copies entry pointers; a row outlives its list only when no null follows the free.

## 4. Verdict

The theory has the ingredients right and the mechanism wrong. Right: the body's Long Bow entry's ExtraDataList was freed under the open loot menu while the server's inventory landed through the engine's RemoveItem/AddObjectToContainer (R13 is the bow in c1; RSI 0x19636C08420, likely the entry, still named it; the list at R14 is dead), and the menu read it through a stale row. Wrong: (1) no InventoryUpdateData was posted for the body on this build (Shows() excludes actors), and a posted null would have PREVENTED the crash, not caused it; (2) the reader is the item card (51897) under the Flash callback RequestItemCardInfo (51130), reached from an SKSE-queued GFx Invoke after the message loop, not UpdateItemList or a row build; (3) r8 = 0x13 is the presence byte index of kEnchantment, the dead pointer is the list's presence bitfield in r9. Hypothesis (confidence med-high on the mechanism, med on the agent): the dead-body diff's RemoveItem (37797 -> 16059, HYPOTHESIS the InventoryChanges removal that frees the worn bow's list and entry, posting dirty only) or the equip handling of its AddObjectToContainer (no message) ran between the menu's kShow build and the first card request, and nothing posted `{body, null}`; the bow's row kept the dead pointers. Falsified if the trace shows a null kInventoryUpdate for the body processed after the freeing call and before the card (then UpdateItemList's incremental path keeps rows it should not), if no SP-originated RemoveItem/AddObjectToContainer/Equip/Unequip touches the body between the last 51144 and the crash (an engine or another plugin's free), or if the crash reproduces with the body's inventory apply held. Implied direction (HYPOTHESIS until the trace): the body's batch needs a null after it (52849 twice, Souls' shape) or must not land under an open menu; the Actor exclusion in Shows() is the gap, not the message.
