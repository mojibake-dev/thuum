## How to read the table

- **Classes** follow the audit's brief:
  - COVERED: every applicable case is asserted on a seat.
  - PARTIAL: at least one case is asserted on a seat.
  - MISSING: no case is asserted on a seat. The only claims are on the actor's own seat or the server's record, live only in a pending scenario, or do not exist.
- **Case marks** in "Asserted?": 1 is the guest's view, 2 is the same object at once, 3 is the other player's host, 4 is distance and terrain, 5 is restart and relaunch.
  - A: asserted on a seat.
  - a: asserted in part, or only as the seat's own log line.
  - s: server record only.
  - p: only in a pending scenario.
  - N: applicable but not asserted.
  - na: not applicable.
  - s and p do not count toward the class.
- **Row order:** MISSING first, then PARTIAL, then COVERED. Within each class, rows are ordered by how often players hit them (high, then medium, then low), and within a band: combat and deaths, loot, NPCs, spells, objects, progression.
- **Open HYPOTHESIS tags** are counted in the verb doc by the brief's rule, with the most player-facing ones named. "Box ticked" means the doc's Status box "T3 scenario green, no HYPOTHESIS tags" is ticked while tags remain open.
- **Reclassified from the readers' calls:**
  - hostility-sync and movement-speed, from PARTIAL to MISSING: their only asserted cases are server records.
  - learned-spells, from COVERED to PARTIAL: the launch leg lives only in a pending scenario.
  - Two rows were split by sub-feature: containers, and the NPC archers and casters.

## Method and limits

- **Readers.** Eleven readers, one per domain, worked from one brief against the frozen snapshot. Their per-verb notes, with cites for every case, are condensed into the table above.
- **Checks on their notes.** A mechanical scan of all 122 scenarios counted the claims per seat, restarts and relaunches, teleport cells and literal `>= 0` claims. I spot-checked against the snapshot every cite in the five above, the god-mode lines, the c-riverwood restatement (git log of that file) and the container default.
- **What counts as a claim.** Only lines inside `assert:` or `until: expect:` count. Screenshots, dump-state, unclaimed log-count steps and "recorded, not claimed" lines do not.
- **What was not read.** No lab run's results, no T0 or T2 suite, and no probe outputs: a probe's green is evidence, not a gate. Frequency is a judgement, not a measurement.
- **Where judgement entered.** It decided where a doc's claimed behaviour makes a case applicable, and how one verb splits into rows. The readers' calls were changed in the four places named under "How to read the table".
