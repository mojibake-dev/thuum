## 1. Scope and sources

Three viewpoints: (a) the PLAYER's own engine, the seat whose character earns skill experience, crosses a level threshold, opens the
Stats menu, picks Health, Magicka or Stamina and spends a perk point; (b) ANOTHER seat's engine, which renders that player as a
figure and receives its hits; (c) a seat that LOADS the character late, at a login after the level-up, a relaunch, a reconnect or a
server restart, and has the record applied.
