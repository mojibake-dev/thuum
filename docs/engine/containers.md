## 1. Scope and sources

Three viewpoints: (a) the OPENER's engine, the seat whose ContainerMenu is open on the container (a chest or a body) and whose own
takes and puts the engine moves; (b) a NON-OPENER's engine, a seat that has the same container loaded with its menu closed, or with its
own menu open on it (then it is an opener for its own engine, with the server's property as the input); (c) a seat that LOADS THE
CONTAINER LATE, at a login, a cell entry, a reconnect or a server restart.

The inputs, where they enter the engine on a seat:
- The player's E. A game-file container, body or item is activation-blocked by the client (objectReferenceEx.ts:67-91, dealWithRef at
  the creation, remoteServer.ts:527), so the engine fires TESActivateEvent without its default processing; the platform's "activate"
  is a sink on that event, not a hook (skyrim-platform EventHandler.cpp:98-133); activationService sends Activate to the server
  (activationService.ts:16, :139-150). The engine's menu opens only from the server's OpenContainer: remoteServer's handler runs
  Papyrus `refr.activate(Game.getPlayer(), true)` (default processing only, remoteServer.ts:257) after the server's inventory property.
- The opener's own take or put: the menu's Flash callbacks (ItemTransfer, EquipItem, TakeAllItems) into the engine's RemoveItem with a
  move-to reference; TESContainerChangedEvent (TESContainerChangedEvent.h:7-17) reaches the platform's "containerChanged"
  (EventHandler.h:483-484, .cpp:375-430) and containersService diffs the player's inventory against its baseline into PutItem or
  TakeItem (containersService.ts:19-99; the baseline at the player's own activation, activationService.ts:19-40).
- Another player's change: the server moves the records (MpObjectReference.cpp:764-800, PutItem and TakeItem) and re-sends the
  "inventory" property to the other occupants (SendInventoryToContainerOccupants :884-897, skipped under two occupants) and as the
  ordinary property broadcast; a refused move resyncs the mover's own inventory and the container's (ResyncAfterRefusedMove :812-822).
  The client applies a property under an open menu at once (formView.ts:817-858 for a reference with a view; remoteServer.ts:1058-1121
  for a game-file container) and otherwise through the queue, one container per update tick, never under an item menu
  (containerApplyQueue.ts:13-58); every apply is sync/inventory.ts applyInventory (:357-380): a diff against the engine's own read
  (getInventory :309-316, the base's TESContainer list plus the ExtraContainerChanges), one TESModPlatform.addItemEx per entry, and on
  the first apply of a base per session resetBase (:335-344: TESModPlatform.resetContainer then removeAllItems).
- A script's AddItem or RemoveItem: the Papyrus natives (ObjectReference.AddItem 56489 and 56490, RemoveItem 56098 with its helper
  56647, RemoveAllItems 56215), which a guest's world scripts reach only through ADR-062's filter (DECISIONS.md:4014-4023).
- The open and the close: the server's OpenContainer (MpObjectReference.cpp:2008-2045: the prune, the exclusive refusal, the occupant,
  SetOpen(true), the inventory to the activator, SendOpenContainer) and the client's second, closing activation when the menu is seen
  closed (remoteServer.ts:330-356); the player's own close (the Flash CloseMenu callback) and the lab's `close-menu {name:
  ContainerMenu}` (TESModPlatform.CloseMenu, PapyrusTESModPlatform.cpp:1043-1048), both a kHide on the UI message queue.
